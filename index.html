Simcenter STAR-CCM+

RotorLab — Rushton turbine stirred-tank benchmark

I built the same baffled tank two ways — steady Moving Reference Frame and fully transient sliding mesh — and both initially over-predicted the published Power Number by 2.1x. This is the story of finding out why.

  • 774,069-cell two-region trimmed mesh; internal in-place imprinted interface
  • Refuted rotor-stator unsteadiness (sliding mesh didn't close the gap) and turbulence bias (wrong order of magnitude)
  • Dimensional audit found a 25% oversized impeller sweep from a CAD primitive convention trap

I predicted the corrected torque analytically before remeshing, then confirmed it experimentally. Both formulations now land within 3% of the published benchmark and 0.74% of each other.

Two closures compared on an identical mesh against the Jovic & Driver / Le, Moin & Kim experimental-DNS benchmark at Reh = 5100 — the case running at the top of this page.

  • Mesh verified before solving: 100% face validity, zero negative-volume cells
  • Reattachment length extracted from wall-shear-stress zero-crossings
  • Realizable k-ε ran low, SST k-ω ran high — bracketing the reference

Published as a full technical report rather than a summary figure.
